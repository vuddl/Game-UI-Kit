# Catalog: game types, art styles, screens

The lists offered in the wireframe questions and the Role B art style question. Always add "Other (type it)" to each list. Use the description of the chosen entry in prompts and in `style/style_guide.md`.

## Game types

| # | Game type | What it is | Reference games |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Puzzle | Observe, reason or arrange to reach a goal: jigsaw, color linking, match-3, pathfinding, logic. | Candy Crush Saga, Royal Match, Monument Valley |
| 2 | Hypercasual / Hybrid casual | Very simple, instantly clear, short sessions. Hybrid casual adds upgrades, collection, missions or character growth for long-term depth. | Stack, Johnny Trigger, Helix Jump, Mob Control |
| 3 | Cozy / Collection | Relaxing, low pressure: caring, gentle exploring and collecting characters, pets or objects, then building a personal space. | Cats & Soup, Neko Atsume, Tsuki Odyssey, Adorable Home |
| 4 | Dressup | Combine outfits, hair, accessories and makeup to express style, meet a theme or compete for a score. | Love Nikki, Life Makeover |
| 5 | Racing / Sport | Racing: driving, speed, overtaking. Sport: simulating sports or managing a team, with skill, tactics and results. | Asphalt Legends, 8 Ball Pool, Forza Horizon 5, The Crew |
| 6 | Pixel | Sharp pixel graphics with a retro feel. A visual style over any genre. | Stardew Valley, Soul Knight, Vampire Survivors, Otherworld Legends |
| 7 | Sandbox | Explore, build, interact and create in a 3D space. | Minecraft, Roblox, Play Together |
| 8 | Action RPG | Real-time combat with direct movement, attacks, dodges and skills, plus growth through levels, gear and upgrades. | Genshin Impact, Punishing: Gray Raven, Diablo Immortal |
| 9 | Tycoon | Run and grow a business or production system: invest, expand, upgrade staff, optimize. | Pizza Ready, My Perfect Hotel, Idle Miner Tycoon, Eatventure |
| 10 | Runner | Continuous movement down a track, dodging obstacles and collecting items by swiping or tapping. | Subway Surfers, Temple Run 2, Talking Tom Gold Run, Sonic Dash |
| 11 | Merge / Decor | Combine items into higher ones; decorate and renovate spaces, often unlocked by merge rewards. | Merge Mansion, Gossip Harbor, Merge Gardens, Homescapes |
| 12 | Farm / Building | Farming, animals, processing and delivery; building and laying out structures or towns, with resource management. | Hay Day, Township, FarmVille 3, SimCity BuildIt |
| 13 | Survival | Simple controls, move to dodge and defeat hordes with auto-attacking weapons, collect EXP to level up and pick skills, waves grow over time. | Survivor.io, Vampire Survivors, Brotato, 20 Minutes Till Dawn |
| 14 | Idle | Progress keeps growing with little input, often with offline rewards; choose upgrades and allocate resources. | AFK Arena, Idle Heroes, Idle Miner Tycoon, Cats & Soup |
| 15 | Card | Cards as actions or units: deck building, effect combos, turn management, including roguelike deckbuilders. | Black Deck, Hearthstone, Anime Deck Battle |
| 16 | Anime | Characters and art inspired by Japanese animation, strong character design, expressions and skill animations. A style over any genre. | Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail, Zenless Zone Zero, Epic Seven |
| 17 | Technology | Robots, machines, AI, future devices or sci-fi as the theme, over any genre. | Mech Arena, War Robots, Battle of Titans |

## Art styles

| # | Art style | UI treatment | Reference games |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pixel | Crisp pixel edges, square or stepped corners, sprite icons, limited palette, bitmap font, high-contrast outlines and per-pixel shadows. | Stardew Valley, Soul Knight |
| 2 | Voxel / Blocky / Utility | Boxy geometry, rectangular panels, grid inventories, direct icons. Function first, modular, little decoration, clean blocky look. | Minecraft, Roblox |
| 3 | 3D Soft Clean | Soft, rounded, slightly raised like plastic toys. Gentle gradients, highlights and soft shadows, little texture, bright colors. | Mob Control, Gecko Go, Animal Party |
| 4 | 3D Low-poly / Minimal Flat | Basic shapes, flat colors, simple icons, few surface effects, clear buttons and cards for quick use. Also called Simple Casual or Flat UI. | Pizza Ready, Play Together |
| 5 | Casual / Playful / Bold Cartoon | Exaggerated shapes, big buttons, bright colors, bold font, characterful icons, clear outlines and hard or medium shadows. Playful is softer; Bold uses strong color blocks and high contrast. | Subway Surfers, Brawl Stars, Rumble Heroes |
| 6 | Outmeta Dark Theme | Dark UI with an older mobile feel: strong gradients, raised buttons, metal borders, heavy shadows and surface texture. Bold text, stat boards and many frames. | Real Steel World Robot Boxing |
| 7 | Chunky Fantasy | Very thick outlines on every part, in dark brown or navy rather than pure black. Almost no sharp corners: large radii, pill and egg shapes. Buttons feel thick, with a drop shadow below and a bright bevel on top. | Clash of Clans, Clash Royale |
| 8 | Soft Casual | Rounded panels, soft buttons, friendly colors, round font, easy item icons. Moderate gradients, highlights and shadows; cheerful reward popups. | Merge Mansion, Gossip Harbor, Royal Match |
| 9 | 2D Hand-painted / Cozy / Pastel | Hand-drawn icons and frames with soft, slightly uneven lines, light colors, gentle contrast, few heavy shadows. Notebook, sticker or everyday-object decoration. | Cats & Soup, Neko Atsume, Adorable Home |
| 10 | Robot / Technology | Modular panels, square or bevelled-corner frames, compact font, technical icons. Deep blue or gray with bright accents, stat bars and gear cards. | Mech Arena |
| 11 | Minimalism / Clean | Only what is needed, organized by space and alignment. Geometric icons, readable font, few accents, borders and effects. Mostly hypercasual. | Stack, Johnny Trigger, Helix Jump |
| 12 | Rustic Casual | Farm and daily life through stylized wood, signboards, paper, baskets and tools. Warm colors, soft shapes, clear produce icons. | Hay Day, Township |
| 13 | Anime | UI frames the characters: compact panels, refined icons, fairly thin frames, standout character cards. Color, pattern and type follow the game's world. | Honkai: Star Rail, Genshin Impact |
| 14 | Modern Minimal | Grid layout, flat or translucent panels, strong typography, minimal icons, controlled accents. Hierarchy by type size, spacing and color blocks instead of frames. | Forza Horizon 5, The Crew, Asphalt Legends |
| 15 | Fantasy | Frames and icons from metal, gems, magic and crests. Dark backgrounds make gold borders, light and skill icons stand out; rarity colors on items. Heroic and premium, decoration varies by screen. | League of Legends, Arena of Valor |

## Screens

Offer these in the wireframe questions, grouped so the list stays readable:

- **Main:** Home, Loading, Ingame, Paused, Stage / Chapter, Tutorial
- **Results:** Victory / Defeat, Level Up, Receive Rewards, Congratulation
- **Progress:** Mission, Daily Login, Battle Pass, Ranking, Stats
- **Collection:** Inventory, Equipment, Character List, Profile
- **Shop and offers:** Shop, Spin, Pack IAP Popup, No Ads Popup
- **Social:** Clan, PvP, Chat
- **System:** Settings, Language, Rate Us
